Our Family's Story

For over three decades, Taquería Doña Alicia has been serving authentic Mexican cuisine with the same love and care that Doña Alicia brought from her hometown in Jalisco.

What started as a small family kitchen has grown into a beloved community gathering place. Every recipe comes from Doña Alicia herself—passed down through generations and perfected over 37 years of serving our neighbors.

We prepare everything fresh daily using traditional methods and the finest ingredients. When you taste our food, you're not just getting a meal—you're experiencing a piece of our family's heritage.

"Cooking is love made visible. Every dish we serve carries the warmth of our family to yours."

- Doña Alicia
